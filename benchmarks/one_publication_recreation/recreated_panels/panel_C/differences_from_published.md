# Panel C — differences from the published figure

Paper: Gorman KB, Williams TD, Fraser WR (2014). Ecological Sexual Dimorphism and Environmental Variability within a Community of Antarctic Penguins (Genus Pygoscelis). PLoS ONE 9(3):e90081. DOI:10.1371/journal.pone.0090081 (article CC BY 4.0; data CC0).

The article does not present a canonical PCA panel of these four morphometrics; this is a NEW visualization derived from the associated data.
