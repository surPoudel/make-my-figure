# Visual QC -- thommen2019_planaria / panel_A

Compared the recreated panel against the published Figure 1B (cropped, stored under CC BY 4.0);
see `side_by_side/thommen2019_planaria.png`.

- **Same kind:** single-panel scatter of body mass on log-log axes with one straight fit line. YES.
- **Point cloud shape:** tight positive linear band with the smallest few points falling *below* the
  line at the bottom-left -- present in both the published panel and the recreation.
- **Publication style:** Publication profile -- despined axes, readable fonts, single accent colour,
  vector PDF/SVG with editable text.
- **Deliberate differences:** the paper uses log-scaled axes with decade ticks (10^-2 .. 10^2) and an
  in-panel "Scaling exponent 1.03 +/- 0.01" annotation; the recreation plots log10-transformed values
  with a title, no in-panel exponent text. Same kind, not pixel-identical.
