## Panels recreated from this dataset

- `penguins_scatter` → scatterplot_with_regression: Fig: bill length vs bill depth by species (Simpson's paradox).
- `penguins_box` → boxplot_or_violin_with_points: Fig: body mass distribution by species with group comparison.
- `penguins_pca` → pca_scatter_from_matrix: Fig: PCA of morphometrics separating species.
- `penguins_heatmap` → heatmap_clustered_matrix: Fig: z-scored morphometric means by species (clustered heatmap).
- `penguins_forest` → forest_plot: Fig: mean body mass by species with 95% CI (forest).
