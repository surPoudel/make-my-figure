# Group-comparison panels in the corpus: how published biology draws them

1398 panels from 116 papers (Cell: 35 papers, Nature: 37 papers, Science: 44 papers). One row per bar/box/violin/dot-strip panel; coded by visual review of web-resolution figures (see visual_review_protocol.md for the UNKNOWN rule). Shares are of coded (non-empty) values.

## Headline

- **all**: individual observations visible in 78% of 1398 group-comparison panels; bar+points 49%, bar without points 13%, box+points 9%, violin+points 3%, dot/strip+summary 14%.
- **Cell**: individual observations visible in 79% of 367 group-comparison panels; bar+points 54%, bar without points 12%, box+points 7%, violin+points 1%, dot/strip+summary 16%.
- **Nature**: individual observations visible in 71% of 451 group-comparison panels; bar+points 38%, bar without points 16%, box+points 13%, violin+points 5%, dot/strip+summary 12%.
- **Science**: individual observations visible in 83% of 580 group-comparison panels; bar+points 55%, bar without points 10%, box+points 7%, violin+points 3%, dot/strip+summary 14%.

## Among bar panels: points over the bar?

- all: 80% of 862 bar panels show the observations.
- Cell: 82% of 241 bar panels show the observations.
- Nature: 70% of 241 bar panels show the observations.
- Science: 84% of 380 bar panels show the observations.

## Reading the numbers

- `unknown`/`UNKNOWN` cells are excluded from shares; low-resolution images limit marker and line judgements.
- Sample sizes are the reviewers' counts of visible points (or the caption's n when stated), so `n_class` is approximate.
- These are observed practices; the publishers' stated requirements are in official_guidelines_audit.csv.
