CLAIMS CHECKED / COULD NOT VERIFY
  - Totals on slide 17 are computed from tutorial/audit/validation/*.json: 12 workflows, 98 checks, 98 captures, all PASS.
  - '39 plot types' / '19 registered tests' come from the running application (registry, statistics/test_registry.py).
    The manuscript text says 38 plot types and 18 statistical procedures (v1.1.0 at submission); the deck describes the
    current build (1.1.1 candidate) and does not attribute its counts to the paper.
  - 'Observations stay visible' controls (size, jitter, fill, edge, arrangement) and experimental presets exist only
    with the presets branch merged into the tutorial branch; they are not in the v1.1.1 candidate on main and are not
    claimed by the manuscript. Backup slide 21 states presets are experimental, not journal endorsements.
  - Figure Package contents on slide 15 are the members of the package written in the validated run
    (audit/validation/figure_package.json), not an inferred list.
  - 'Browser version' on slide 4: apps/streamlit_app exists in the repository (Streamlit); not claimed to be feature-complete.
  - Not verified and therefore not claimed: any performance figure beyond Fig. 4E as drawn; macOS testing (manuscript says
    macOS was not tested); 'publication-ready' is used only as 'publication-style'.
  - 'What comes next' (slide 18) is limited to the tutorial plan (MAINTAINING_THE_TUTORIAL.md) and the manuscript
    Discussion's stated principle and Builder limitation; no roadmap items were invented. The brief's third area
    ('researcher evaluation') is not documented in the manuscript or project and was replaced by the manuscript's own
    stated principle for future development.
